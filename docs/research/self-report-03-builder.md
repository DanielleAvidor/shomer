# Self-report 03: the builder (the family member who gets asked)

**Date:** 2026-11-21. Written from the builder's own answers. She is also the author of the product, so this is a point of view from the family "checker", not an independent user.

- Family members send her a suspicious message about once a month.
- She also receives a suspicious message herself every one to two months and checks it herself.
- How she checks: sends it to an AI chat, compares the address with the original address of the real site, and logs into her own account on the real site to see whether the notice exists.
- Wish: a tool that does this verification automatically. Discussed and narrowed (see PRD section 13): no stored passwords and no agent logged in to accounts; instead, check the address against the official one, ask a context question, and open the official site so the person signs in herself.
