# Threat landscape: how big is the danger?

Desk research, 2026-11-27. Paraphrased from the sources linked; figures are as reported there. Confidence: **High** = official body or primary data. **Medium** = official data reported by media, or a survey with a small or online-only sample. **Low** = a single outlet, or an allegation.

## In Israel

| Finding | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| In a National Cyber Directorate (INCD) public survey (October 2026, 507 internet users aged 18+, run by Geocartography), 67% had received a suspicious text message in the past month. About half had been exposed to a cyber attack in the past year, and only about a third said they know how to respond. | [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-914785) | Medium (online sample, so older people who are not online are under-represented) |

These are reports, not confirmed victims. | [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-887964), [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20028307) | Medium-high |

| Older people specifically: police reportedly arrested five suspects in an alleged network that stole over 3 million NIS from dozens of older people, using fake debt messages with official-looking links (over 30 victims identified so far). | [Babli, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.babli.co.il/polise-and-criminals/tkoq4s) | Low (single outlet; allegations) |

## What this means for Shomer

1. **The threat is large and recurring in Israel**, and it comes in waves that copy the organisations people deal with every day (Israel Post, police, Tax Authority, gov.il).
2. **The damage is concentrated** (US data). A few large losses make most of the money. The goal is to prevent the rare costly mistake, not to filter every message. This matches the low frequency people describe in my conversations.
3. **Real and fake messages now coexist** (police SMS tickets). "Ignore everything" and "trust the format" both fail. A check against the official address is what separates them, and a check of the address alone is still not proof that a message is real. That is why Shomer never says "safe".
4. **Existing advice assumes suspicion.** The INCD and police advice (check yourself, use the official site) works for a person who already doubts the message. See the synthesis for why that is not the whole population.
